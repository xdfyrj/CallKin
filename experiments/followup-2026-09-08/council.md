# Council: first follow-up experiment

Mode: three independent LLM delegates, blind first round, sequential cross-examination, final positions. Personas: Aristotle (definitions), Ada (invariants), Feynman (discriminating tests). This is methodological review by agents, not external human verification.

## Verdict
Freeze48 audit groups (30 seeded random,18 purposeful), full target universes from the three already-exposed binaries, named token+CFG intersection versus token+CFG+relation intersection, k16 primary and k8/32 sensitivity. Keep F6 and its whole-queue10k/500m budget gate unchanged. Exact normalized instruction-body and V0 are references; Rescue16 is secondary. Price before expensive comparison.

## Corrections reached through cross-examination
- Exact baseline means instruction bodies, not symbol equality.
- Current problem is same-source grouping within each retained binary, not cross-build clone search.
- No subset of these already-exposed programs becomes a novel-source holdout by being renamed.
- Query_count times k is not a safe cost bound: selection is symmetric, combines views and induces complete-link comparisons.
- Do not introduce arbitrary improvement thresholds or change the current positive/neutral policy during the audit.
- Source consistency, exact compiler-origin mapping and external human reproduction are different evidence levels.

## Unresolved counterfactual
C3 is a subset of C2. Better final quality can arise from pruning candidate edges, changing complete-link ordering or enabling a run under the budget. A selectivity-matched negative control is still needed before crediting relation-specific information. This first study reports the effect of the current relation-consensus policy only.

## Initial actions
Commit protocol and sample before new inference; verify named-view projection against direct generation; audit actual source definitions separately from predictions; record refused runs as incomplete, not zero-quality results.
